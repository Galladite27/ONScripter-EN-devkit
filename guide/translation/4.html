ONScripter-EN translation-focused quick-start guide: 4 - replacing audio & video

Locating and replacing audio & video

As with images, while the game resources are extracted from the game archive, you can browse through the files to find audio & video files which need translating, edit them, and then replace the originals with the new versions. Use common sense when deciding what should be kept the same, such as volume or length (if the file is timed to match some other file's length or a hard-coded delay).

Warning

Due to a bug in the SDL_mixer library, an increased or decreased sample rate can affect the pitch of audio. Ensure that you keep the sample rate of your replacement audio files the same as that of the old ones.
